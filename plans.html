FAQ

Good questions

What counts as a request?add

Anything web: a new page, a copy change, a promo landing page, a speed fix, a new form. Bigger projects get split into smaller requests and worked through the queue one at a time.

How fast is turnaround, really?add

Most requests get a first pass within your plan's turnaround, counted in working days. Bigger requests are broken into steps, so you see progress every few days instead of waiting weeks.

Can I pause my plan?add

Yes. Pause whenever the queue is empty. Your site stays hosted and maintained, and unused days in your billing cycle carry over when you resume.

Is there a contract?add

No. Plans are month to month. Cancel anytime before your next billing cycle.

Do I own my website?add

Yes. The design, code and content are yours. If you leave, I hand everything over and help move it to your new host.

Can I switch plans?add

Anytime. Upgrades start right away; downgrades take effect at your next billing date.

What do you build with?add

Fast, modern stacks chosen per project — static sites and TypeScript front ends, with Node.js, ASP.NET Core, Python and SQL behind them when a project needs real backend work.
